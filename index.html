How to Play

Connect adjacent numbers in the 5×5 grid using math operators to equal one of three target numbers.

Rules

  • Tap a number to start your path
  • Tap any of the 8 surrounding numbers to extend it
  • Tap the operator between numbers to cycle: + − × ÷
  • Tap the dim ( ) buttons around any number to toggle parentheses
  • Each number cell can only be used once per path
  • Division must produce a whole number
  • Without parens, expressions evaluate left to right; parens change grouping

Example

3 × 7 − 6 = 15

3 × 7 = 21, then 21 − 6 = 15

2-Player Rules

Players take turns. Claim a target on your turn by submitting a valid expression. First to claim 2 out of 3 targets wins. Press Pass Turn if you're stuck.
